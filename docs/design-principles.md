# Design principles

1. **The answer is the lecturer.** An answer starts with the original moment at the cited second, not a paraphrase.
2. **Your data stays yours.** Everything runs on the device by default. No account. Anything that leaves the device is optional and labelled.
3. **Honest about what works.** The app says plainly which links open instantly and which need a transcript.
4. **Legal by construction.** No downloading or scraping. Every pack carries its source and license.
5. **Works on cheap hardware.** Chromebooks, budget phones and slow connections are the target, not an afterthought.
6. **Calm and premium.** Black, white and charcoal. Few colors, clear type, no clutter.
7. **Accessible from the start.** Keyboard, screen readers, contrast and reduced motion are part of every change.
